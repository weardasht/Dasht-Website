Why Dasht?

دشت

There's no shortage of Urdu brands right now — but most of them turn the volume all the way up. Heavy calligraphy across the whole chest, three lines of poetry, loud colour. Meanwhile the t-shirt you actually reach for every day is almost always in English.

We started Dasht because we wanted both at once: Urdu that fits into everyday life, printed clean and simple on heavyweight cotton — for everyone who loves the language but wants their t-shirt to stay out of the way.

Shipping Policy

All orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery across Pakistan takes 3–5 business days depending on your city.

A flat shipping charge of Rs. 250 applies to orders under Rs. 10,000. Orders of Rs. 10,000 or more ship free.

Cash on delivery is available nationwide — pay in cash when your order arrives. Once your order ships, we'll call to confirm delivery details.

Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to love your Dasht tee. If something's wrong — wrong size, damaged in transit, or not what you expected — let us know within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange an exchange or refund.

Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with tags attached.

To start a return, contact us with your order number and reason for return.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to process your order: your name, phone number, delivery address, and city. This information is used solely to fulfil and confirm your order.

If you join our VIP list, we'll store your email to send occasional updates about new drops. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

We do not sell or share your information with third parties beyond what's needed to deliver your order.
